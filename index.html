    The number box is the refractive index. Change it to match your own sputtered films. M (middle index) is optional: it only appears in a design when you write an M in the formula, like H M L.

    Stack

    The formula is only a quick way to sketch a starting stack: H, M and L are quarter-waves at λ₀, 0.5L half that, (HL)^5 repeats 5 times. Every layer is converted to a physical thickness in nm, which is what the table, optimizer and recipe use.

      Targets and optimization

      SpecFrom (nm)To (nm)%

      Specs: T or R, at or above (≥) or at or below (≤) the %. “avg” checks the average over the range, otherwise every wavelength must meet it. Optimize adjusts each layer’s physical thickness. With “Add and remove layers” on, it adds a pair wherever that helps most until every target passes, and drops layers that shrink to nothing. Leave a gap between pass and block zones for the edge.

      Plot range

      R specs are always the coated side alone, the way a reflection scan measures it. T specs include the back surface as set here.
